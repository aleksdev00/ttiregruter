A1: GROWTH.CO RECRUITING GROUP
A2: CDL Class A Driver Recruitment Script (Training Guide for New Recruiters)
A3: Goal
A6: Build rapport, qualify the driver, understand their needs, and match them with the best company position or lease-purchase opportunity.
A9: STEP 1: INTRODUCTION
A12: "Hello, is this [Driver Name]?
A14: My name is [Recruiter Name], and I'm calling from GROWTH.CO Recruiting Group. We specialize in helping CDL Class A drivers find the best opportunities based on their experience, income goals, home time preferences, and career plans.
A16: Do you have a few minutes to discuss what you're looking for in your next driving position?"
A18: STEP 2: BUILD RAPPORT
A20: "Before we talk about opportunities, I'd like to learn more about you so I can find the best fit possible.
A22: How long have you been driving commercially?"
A24: Listen carefully and keep the conversation natural.
A26: Additional questions:
A28: What type of equipment have you driven?
A29: Dry van, reefer, flatbed, tanker, intermodal?
A30: What states do you usually run?
A31: What do you enjoy most about trucking?
A32: What are you hoping to improve with your next opportunity?
A33: STEP 3: QUALIFY THE DRIVER
A34: Experience
A36: "How much verifiable CDL Class A experience do you currently have?"
A38: License Status
A40: "Is your CDL currently valid and active?"
A42: Safety Record
A44: "Any major accidents in the last 3 years?"
A46: "Any moving violations recently?"
A48: Employment Status
A50: "Are you currently employed, or are you looking for something new?"
A52: Drug Test
A54: "Would you be able to pass a DOT drug screen?"
A56: STEP 4: IDENTIFY THEIR GOALS
A58: This is the most important part.
A60: Ask:
A62: Income
A64: "What kind of weekly income are you looking for?"
A66: Home Time
A68: "How often do you want to be home?"
A70: Examples:
A72: Every weekend
A73: Every other weekend
A74: 2 weeks out / 2 days home
A75: OTR lifestyle
A76: Freight Preference
A78: "What type of freight do you prefer?"
A80: Company vs Lease Purchase
A82: "Are you looking for a company driver position, or have you considered lease purchase opportunities?"
A84: If unsure:
A86: "No worries. I can explain both options and help determine what would make the most sense for your situation."
A88: STEP 5: EDUCATE THE DRIVER
A89: COMPANY POSITION EXPLANATION
A91: "With a company position, you'll receive a steady paycheck, benefits, and lower financial risk. The company covers most operational expenses and maintenance."
A93: Benefits
A94: Stable income
A95: Health benefits
A96: Paid orientation
A97: Lower risk
A98: No truck expenses
A101: LEASE PURCHASE EXPLANATION
A103: "With lease purchase, you're working toward ownership of your truck. Earnings can be significantly higher, but you'll also have more responsibility and expenses."
A105: Benefits
A106: Higher earning potential
A107: Path to truck ownership
A108: More independence
A109: Business-building opportunity
A110: Important
A112: "We only recommend lease purchase if it aligns with your goals and financial situation."
A114: STEP 6: PRESENT THE VALUE OF GROWTH.CO
A116: "At GROWTH.CO Recruiting Group, we're not tied to just one carrier.
A118: Our job is to learn what matters most to you and connect you with the company that best fits your needs.
A120: Whether you're looking for:
A122: Better pay
A123: More home time
A124: Newer equipment
A125: Lease purchase
A126: Regional routes
A127: OTR opportunities
A129: We'll help you compare options and find the right fit."
A131: STEP 7: OVERCOME COMMON OBJECTIONS
A132: "I'm happy where I am."
A134: "I completely understand. Many drivers we work with felt the same way initially. Out of curiosity, if a better opportunity came along with more pay or better home time, would you be open to hearing about it?"
A136: "I'm not looking right now."
A138: "No problem at all. Things change quickly in trucking. Would it be okay if I kept your information and reached out when a particularly strong opportunity becomes available?"
A140: "I've heard bad things about lease purchase."
A142: "That's understandable. There are good and bad programs in the industry. That's why we carefully review opportunities and only present options that make sense for each driver's goals."
A144: STEP 8: CLOSE THE DRIVER
A146: "Based on everything you've shared, I believe I have a few opportunities that could be a strong fit for you.
A148: The next step would be gathering a few details and getting your profile submitted for review.
A150: If everything looks good, we can start setting up interviews and moving toward orientation."

A176: ✅ Recent Accidents/Violations

A180: POWERFUL CLOSING STATEMENT
A182: "At GROWTH.CO Recruiting Group, we're not just filling seats. We're helping professional drivers find opportunities that support their goals, families, and future. My job is to make sure you're in the best possible position to succeed."
A184: GOLDEN RULE FOR NEW RECRUITERS
A186: Don't sell a job.
A187: Discover the driver's needs first.
A189: The more questions you ask, the easier it becomes to find the right company and build trust with the driver. Trust closes more drivers than any sales pitch ever will.
