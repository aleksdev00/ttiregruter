# TTI Recruiting — Driver FAQ Answer Bank

**Purpose:** Ready-to-say, driver-facing answers for recruiter calls. Answers are in natural U.S. English because TTI recruits drivers in the United States.

**Important:** TTI is a recruiting firm connecting CDL-A drivers with motor carriers; the carrier makes the final hiring decision. Carrier-specific pay, routes, home time, equipment, benefits, costs, openings, eligibility, orientation, and timing have not been verified here. Replace every bracketed field with current written carrier information before using it. If a detail is unknown, use the “I’ll verify it” answer instead of guessing. Do not promise a job, pay level, miles, home time, bonus, or approval.

**Known offer notes to use carefully:** BBCL has confirmed it needs drivers, but its driver-facing terms are not yet verified. The latest TTI note for Yankee says a one-year experience minimum and a 2023 Volvo was noted as available; confirm that the opening and truck are still available before presenting them.

**Call style:** Answer the question directly, explain the verified detail in plain language, then ask one relevant follow-up. Don’t read the whole FAQ. Use only the section that applies to this driver and this offer.
